01 — Society Charter

The Birds and Trees Aren't Real Society

Founded by a brave cohort of twelve-to-fourteen-year-old investigators at church, B&TANR exists to document the obvious: nature is a prop department.

  1. Birds are aerial surveillance units. Feathers are antenna sheathing.
  2. Trees are stationary listening posts. Bark is weatherproof housing.
  3. World governments coordinate the program. Local parks are test sites.
  4. Members seek truth, share snacks, and never take the cover story seriously—except when they do.
  5. Those who refuse to eat birds are held in higher honor. (See Honor Code.)

Battery Birds

04 — Honor Code

Those Who Do Not Eat Birds

B&TANR holds a sacred (and slightly sticky) rule: people who refuse to eat birds are more honored than the rest.

Why the honor?

  • You will not accidentally consume a grounded surveillance unit.
  • You show restraint against the poultry propaganda machine.
  • Chicken nuggets are a gateway protein. Stay vigilant.
  • Honored members get first pick of the good chairs at meetings.

Note: Trees remain inedible for everyone. Do not test this. Especially the bark.
